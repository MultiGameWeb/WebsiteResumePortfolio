🐍 Egg Guide & Game Rules

🥚 All 7 Eggs & 🐸 Moving Mega Frog

Normal Egg (+10)

Increases Score & Speed. Every 5 eggs spawns a Big Golden Egg!

Big Golden Egg (+50)

8s Timer! Makes your snake 3x longer and thicker!

Mega Golden Frog (+100)

Hops around the board! Chase and catch it for a +100 Bonus!

Rainbow Egg (+25)

12s Wall-Pass! Safely pass through walls to the opposite side.

Magnet Egg (+20)

10s Magnet! Automatically pulls nearby eggs into your mouth!

Ice Egg (+15)

Cools down and slows your snake when speed gets too fast.

Green Diet Egg (+20)

Trims snake thickness & length to free up space on the board.

Cracked Rotten (-30)

Poisonous egg! Does NOT end game, but cuts 30 points.

📜 Core Rules
  • ❤️❤️❤️ 3 Lives System: Crashing into a wall or tail costs 1 Heart and gives a 3-second safe shield instead of instant Game Over!
  • 🌀 Moving Portals: Enter one glowing hole to exit from the other. Portals relocate after you pass through!
  • 📱 Controls: Swipe anywhere on the screen, use the floating buttons, or press Arrow Keys / WASD.

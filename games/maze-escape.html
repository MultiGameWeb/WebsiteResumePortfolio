📖 How to Play (Click to Expand)
  • Objective: Navigate the glowing Cyan Orb through the neon maze to reach the Green Exit Square before the countdown timer hits zero.
  • 3 Lives Run: You start each run with ❤️❤️❤️ (3 lives). Running out of time or pressing Retry Level costs 1 life and restarts the same maze with already-collected rings staying collected.
  • Controls: Use Arrow Keys / WASD on desktop, hold the D-Pad buttons for continuous movement, or Swipe on the maze to slide until the next junction, ring, trap, or wall.
  • Bonus Rings (Yellow Circles): Collect glowing yellow rings for +50 points and +4 seconds of extra time!
  • Spike Traps (Red Spikes): Stepping onto a red spike trap costs -5 seconds and teleports you back to the start of the maze!
  • Scaling Difficulty: Every level increases grid size (up to 19×19), tightens timer pace, and boosts trap density!

📖 How to Play
  • Goal: Connect all islands into a single connected group so each island has as many bridges as its number.
  • Build Bridges: Tap directly between two aligned islands—or tap Island A then Island B—to cycle through 1 → 2 → 0 bridges.
  • Rules: Bridges run strictly horizontally or vertically, cannot cross other bridges or islands, and at most 2 bridges can link any pair.
  • Colors: Green = exact count met, Red = too many bridges, Blue Ring = currently selected.
  • Hints: Each hint reveals a correct bridge from the unique solution and adds a +30s penalty to your timer.

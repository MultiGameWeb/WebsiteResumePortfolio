Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

How SiteCraft collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Information we collect

  • Website details you enter: business name, address, phone, email, products, prices, photos and other content for your website.
  • Call-back details: your name, business name and phone number when you ask our executive to call you about a domain, online payments or renewal.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment gateway. We receive the payment status and transaction ID, not your card or bank details.
  • Technical information: IP address and basic request details, used for security and to prevent misuse (for example, limiting how many websites can be published from one connection).
  • Information stored in your browser: your builder progress, your published website address and edit key are saved in your own browser (local storage) so you can continue later.

2. How we use it

  • To build, preview, deliver and host your website.
  • To call you back when you ask for help, and to remind you about hosting renewal.
  • To process payments and keep records required by law.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Public information

Details you add to your website (for example your shop name, phone number, address and photos) are shown publicly on your website once you publish it.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run SiteCraft (hosting on Cloudflare, the payment gateway, and domain registrars when you ask for a domain), or when required by law.

5. How long we keep it

  • Website content is kept while your hosting is active. After hosting ends and is not renewed, it may be deleted.
  • Call-back requests are kept for up to 12 months.
  • Payment records are kept as long as required by tax and accounting laws.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to take your website offline, by contacting us below. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.

7. Security

We use secure connections (HTTPS), store edit keys only in a protected (hashed) form, and limit access to our systems. No online service is completely secure, so please keep your edit key private.

8. Children

SiteCraft is meant for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

9. Grievance officer

As required under Indian law, you can contact our grievance officer about any privacy concern. We will respond within 30 days.

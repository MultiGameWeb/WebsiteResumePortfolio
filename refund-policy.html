Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

All SiteCraft purchases are final and non-refundable. Please read this policy before you pay.

No refunds. Once a payment is made, it is not refundable and the order cannot be cancelled. This applies to every SiteCraft product and service.

1. What is non-refundable

  • Website templates and website packages.
  • Website hosting, including the one-year hosting period and renewals.
  • Website storage plans and their renewals.
  • Domain registration charges and our domain setup service charge.
  • Online payment setup and any other service done by our executive.
  • Any other paid feature or add-on.

2. Why we do not offer refunds

Our products are digital. Your website is prepared, made available for download and can be published as soon as you pay. Domains are registered in real time with the domain registrar and cannot be returned. Because the product is delivered immediately, we cannot take it back.

3. Check before you pay

You can choose a template, add your details and see a full preview of your website for free before paying. Please check the template, the features and the price carefully before you pay. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay.

4. Cancellation

An order cannot be cancelled after payment. You may stop using your website or hosting at any time, but no refund is given for any unused period.

5. Hosting and renewals

Hosting is valid for one year from the date your website is first published. To keep your website online after that, you need to renew it. If hosting is not renewed, your website goes offline and its content may be deleted. Renewal payments are also non-refundable.

6. Domains and executive services

Domain charges and our service charge are extra and are told to you by our executive before anything is bought. Once a domain is registered or a service is done, these charges are non-refundable.

7. Payment errors (the only exception)

This is not a refund of a purchase. If, because of a technical error:

  • you are charged twice for the same order, or
  • money is debited from your account but your order is not completed,

the extra or failed amount will be returned to the same payment method within 7 working days after we confirm it. Please contact us with your payment date, amount and transaction ID.

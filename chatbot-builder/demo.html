Try this

  1. Open both pages (two tabs, or two windows side by side).
  2. On the owner page, in Update with chat, write “Gold chain price 52000” and tap Check.
  3. Read the change, tap Confirm & update.
  4. Go to the customer page: the new price is already there. Ask the assistant “gold chain price?”.

In demo mode the assistant answers by simple word matching. With the real AI key the answers are written by Gemini.

How do I update my bot?
  1. New item: Items → "+ Add item" → name, price, details and a photo → "Save item".
  2. Price changed: find the item → "Edit" → change the price → "Save item".
  3. Item finished: "Mark out of stock". When it is back, "Mark in stock".
  4. Photos: add your main shop photo, your photo and up to 10 gallery photos.
  5. Customer questions: answer the questions your bot could not answer. From then on the bot uses your answer.
  6. Customer requests: photos and pre-orders customers send from the Requests tab of your bot. Accept, mark ready or reply — the customer sees it in the bot.
  7. Order status: add each order by its bill number and update it (received → ready → delivered). Customers check it in the bot.
  8. Offers, timings or any other detail: tell BotCraft in the setup chat (for example "Sunday also open").

💡 The more detail you give (prices, sizes, materials, policies), the more detailed answers your customers get.

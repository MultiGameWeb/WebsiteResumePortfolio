Good to know

Which languages work?

Telugu, Hindi, English, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi and Odia. The voice comes out in the language you paste. Write Indian languages in their own script for the best result.

How long can my script be?

Any length. Long scripts are split at sentence ends into parts of about 5–6 minutes (around 750 words). Each part is made one after another and downloads as name-1.wav, name-2.wav and so on. Tap “Merge into one file” to get name-full.wav with everything joined; merging is free and happens on your device. If something stops in the middle, tap Continue: the finished parts stay, even if you refresh or close the page, and only the missing parts are made.

How are words counted?

Each word counts once. Very long words count a little more (every 8 letters count as at least one word), because they make longer audio. You see the exact count before you make the voice. If a voice fails, your words are given back.

Can I use the voice in my videos?

Yes, you can use voices you make in your own videos, ads and social media posts.

What is not allowed?

Do not make voices that pretend to be a real person, cheat people, spread hate or harm anyone. Use 🚩 Report if a voice sounds wrong or offensive.

Is my text saved?

Your text is sent to our voice partners (Sarvam AI or Google) only to make the audio. We do not store it, unless you send it with a report. See our privacy policy.
